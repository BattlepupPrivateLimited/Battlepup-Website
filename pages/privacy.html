The only thing we ask you for is your age. We collect data about how you play our games so we can fix and improve them. We do not sell your information and we do not deal with data brokers. For market research we use only publicly available information.

2.2 Gameplay and behaviour data

While you play, our games record data about how you play. This can include:

  • progress, levels, modes and items you use;
  • choices and actions you take in the game;
  • how long and how often you play;
  • settings you choose;
  • where players get stuck, quit or run into problems.

This data is linked to [player ID / device identifier] and not to your name or email address, because we do not ask for either. Even so, because it is linked to an identifier, we treat it as personal information and protect it accordingly.

2.3 Technical data

When a game runs, we may receive technical details such as your device type, operating system, game version, language setting and crash reports. Your IP address may be received by our servers or service providers when your device connects to them.

2.4 Messages you send us

If you email us, call us or message us on social media, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your email address, your name and what you write. We use this only to reply to you.

2.5 Website visits

This website is a static website. Our hosting provider, [Github Pages], may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. To display our fonts, the website loads them from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google

2.6 What we do not collect

In our games and on this website we do not ask for your name, email address, home address, phone number or payment details. Payments are handled by the store or platform where you buy a game.

  • understand how people play, so we can balance, fix and improve our games;
  • find and fix bugs and crashes;
  • keep our games and website secure and fair;
  • reply to your messages;
  • follow the law.

10. International transfers

We currently store and process your personal data on local, secure servers within [India].

Future Cloud & International Transfers

In the future, we may migrate or back up personal data to secure cloud infrastructure or third-party service providers located outside your home country, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK).

Whenever your personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded to it by implementing at least one of the following legal safeguards:

Adequacy Decisions: We transfer data to countries that have been deemed to provide an adequate level of protection for personal data by the European Commission or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Where we use service providers outside the EEA or UK (including US-based cloud hosting providers), we implement approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. These clauses require service providers to protect your data to the same legal standards required within the EU and UK.

Data Privacy Frameworks: Where applicable, we partner with US-based cloud and service providers certified under the EU-U.S. and UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks.

Analytics & Third Parties

When sharing data with external analytics partners or game balancing consultants, we ensure that data is fully anonymized or key-coded (pseudonymized) before transfer. Fully anonymized data—where an individual can no longer be identified—is not considered personal data under data protection laws.
